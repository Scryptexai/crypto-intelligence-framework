# LayerZero Labs: Financial Profile (Funding & Revenue Economics)

## TL;DR
- LayerZero Labs raised **$263 million in priced equity across four rounds** (Seed $2M → Series A $6–6.3M → Series A+ $135M at $1B → Series B $120M at $3B), and has **not raised a new priced equity round since April 2023**; instead it has layered on four token/M&A capital events — a16z's $55M ZRO purchase (April 2025), the Stargate protocol acquisition (Aug 2025), a ~$150M buyback of 50M ZRO from early investors (Sept 2025), and undisclosed-size strategic investments from Tether, Citadel Securities and ARK Invest (Feb 10, 2026).
- The **FTX settlement (Jan 31, 2025) dollar figure was never publicly disclosed** — LayerZero said only that the "original repurchase" was returned to the estate — and the **"$111 million" figure is not supported by any source** and should be dropped. The company self-reports being cash-rich (~$134M as of late 2022) with a claimed 7-year runway, but no newer treasury balance has been disclosed.
- The **LayerZero Foundation's protocol revenue is effectively zero today** because the fee switch is not yet live; messaging fees (over $11.5M in Q1'24 per Messari; ~$3.6M annualized per DefiLlama) flow to third-party DVNs/Executors, not to LayerZero. The only cash flow reaching the token is Stargate revenue funding ZRO buybacks, and LayerZero Labs says it funds itself entirely through off-chain commercial services and "has never had to sell a token to fund itself."

### 3. FTX litigation & settlement
- **The suit:** FTX Trading Ltd. / Maclaurin Investments Ltd. (f/k/a Alameda Ventures) / West Realm Shires Services v. LayerZero Labs Ltd., Ari Litan, and Skip & Goose LLC — Adv. Pro. No. 23-50492-JTD (Bankruptcy Court, D. Del.; main case 22-11068-JTD), complaint filed September 2023. Sought **$21.37M** preference (90-day pre-bankruptcy withdrawals) from LayerZero Labs, **$13.07M** from former COO Ari Litan, and **$6.65M** from subsidiary Skip & Goose LLC. The Block framed the total as recovering **$86M** transferred on the eve of bankruptcy; Law360 framed the total suit as seeking "more than $100 million." [Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/1720010/ftx-sues-to-claw-back-100m-from-virgin-islands-firm) Underlying deals: Alameda paid >$70M for a ~4.92% stake; a $45M LayerZero→Alameda loan at 8% interest; $25M for 100M STG tokens (later to be repurchased for ~$10M, never completed).
- **Settlement — January 31, 2025.** Confirmed by CEO Bryan Pellegrino on X and reported by The Block, Cointelegraph, and Invezz. **The final settlement dollar amount was never publicly disclosed.** Pellegrino wrote: "After more than two long years and millions in legal fees (lawyers always win) we have reached a settlement agreement with the FTX estate… Original repurchase has been returned to the estate." Structurally, LayerZero "bought FTX/FTX Ventures/Alameda out of 100% of their equity position, token warrants, and any and all agreements." [Coinspeaker](https://www.coinspeaker.com/layerzero-ceo-announces-settlement-ftx-estate/) Per LayerZero's 2026 ZRO-token blog, the original TGE allocation included **40M ZRO repurchased from FTX/Alameda**, which the Foundation "returned to strategic partners as part of the legal settlement."
- **The "$111 million" figure is NOT supported by any source** — confirmed via a dedicated docket/press search. The figures in the record are $21.37M, $86M, and "over $100M" (Law360), plus the *separate* $107M cash-balance figure (unrelated to any payment). No stipulation of dismissal or Rule 9019 motion disclosing a dollar amount is publicly retrievable; the last confirmed docket entry before the settlement was Doc 84 (Jan 22, 2025). [Epiq11](https://document.epiq11.com/document/getdocumentbycode?docId=4429948&projectCode=FTX) The only primary source for the settlement is Pellegrino's own (self-interested) X post — treat the terms as confidential/undisclosed.

### 4. Settlement funding source & treasury/runway impact
- **No new round was raised to fund the settlement.** LayerZero was self-described as cash-rich, and the settlement was structured as a token/equity buyout (returning the "original repurchase" plus 40M ZRO to strategic partners) rather than a large disclosed cash outlay. The one measurable cost repeatedly cited is "millions in legal fees" over the 2+ year process — but **no specific legal-fee total was disclosed**, and no dollar impact on runway was quantified.

### 5. Treasury figures
- **November 10–14, 2022 (via The Block / CoinDesk / T-Net):** ~**$134M** in liquid assets, ~90% cash/stablecoins, broken down as **$107M direct cash + $27M on-chain**, with **$11.5M trapped on FTX** (treated as $0 for planning). Claimed runway of "no less than 7 years" under aggressive assumptions. [CoinGape](https://coingape.com/layerzero-ceo-confirms-settlement-ftx-estate-zro-price/)
- **January 31, 2025:** Pellegrino re-cited the same 2022 figures ($107M cash + $27M on-chain, 90% stablecoins) [Mitrade](https://www.mitrade.com/insights/news/live-news/article-3-613917-20250201) — **no newer treasury figure was disclosed.** No independently verified 2023–2026 treasury balance exists in public sources.
- LayerZero Labs's June 2026 ZRO-token blog states Labs "funds all development of LayerZero and Zero through offchain operations and has never had to sell a token to fund itself," and deploys "a portion of its operating earnings" into ZRO buybacks — implying operating profitability, but **no balance sheet is disclosed**.

### 6. Revenue model & figures
- **Protocol mechanics:** Every LayerZero message pays **DVN (verification) fees and Executor fees** to independent third-party operators. **LayerZero takes a 0% protocol take rate on messaging fees** (per DefiLlama's fee adapter). A **fee switch**, when activated via governance, would apply an additional protocol fee whose proceeds buy back and burn ZRO. Three fee referendums have been held (approval >96% each); turnout was 10.96% (Dec 2024), 13.01% (June 2025), and 3.71% (Dec 2025). **The fee switch is not yet live**, so the LayerZero Foundation's protocol revenue is effectively **zero**.
- **Disclosed fee figures:**
  - Messari ("State of LayerZero Q1 2024"): DVN + Executor fee revenue "grew 31% QoQ to over **$11.5 million in Q1'24**. The top source chains for revenue were Arbitrum ($3.4 million), BNB Chain ($2.4 million), Optimism ($2.1 million), and Polygon ($2.1 million)" — but this flows to DVNs/Executors, not the Foundation.
  - DefiLlama currently shows ~**$3.59M** in fee revenue (annualized), again flowing to external DVN/Executor participants.
  - Scale context: cumulative >$225–260B value transferred across 165 chains; 159–160M messages processed.
- **Stargate revenue → ZRO buybacks:** Post-acquisition, 50% of Stargate revenue funded ZRO buybacks for six months, moving to 100% from March/April 2026. **Sept–Nov 2025: Stargate generated $2.4M revenue, of which $1.2M bought ZRO on the open market** (LayerZero blog). The Foundation's buyback tracker shows ~1.9M ZRO bought back at ~$2.8M cost from Stargate revenue to date. Analyst estimates of Stargate's run-rate: ~$5.4M/year (OAK Research), with DefiLlama's 2025 fee estimate at $2.15M, down from $11.5M in 2024 and $20.3M in 2023.
- **LayerZero Labs commercial revenue lines** (per company blog, no figures given): Executor/gas-abstraction margins, chain-integration services, a Value Transfer API charging basis points on token movement, and "Console" annual subscriptions for issuers/tokenization platforms.
- **Total buybacks:** Per the company blog, "LayerZero entities have deployed **$112.7M into ZRO buybacks since September 2025**," including the 50M-ZRO strategic-partner buyback and the $10M November 2025 Labs open-market purchase; "Institutional purchases, Foundation, and Labs buybacks now represent 19.77% of total supply."
- Third-party data-aggregator revenue estimates (Getlatka: $33.4M 2025 revenue; ZoomInfo: $1M–$5M) are **unverified and internally inconsistent — treat with skepticism.**

### 7. Burn rate / headcount
- **Headcount:** ~150 employees (TrueUp; Not Boring, Dec 2023); **Fortune (Feb 2026) describes a "165-person company."** Getlatka cites 151. Data aggregators (ZoomInfo, Tracxn) give wildly inconsistent counts (6–10, 11, 51–200) and should not be relied upon.
- **Operating burn rate / opex: not disclosed.** No public source quantifies monthly burn.

### 8. Token sale structure (funding economics only)
- **June 20, 2024 "Proof-of-Donation" ZRO claim:** To claim ZRO, users donated **$0.10 per ZRO** in USDC/USDT/ETH to Protocol Guild (an Ethereum core-developer funding collective), with the LayerZero Foundation **matching donations up to $10M**, for a projected total of **~$18.5M** to Protocol Guild (per the LayerZero Foundation and The Block). 85M ZRO (8.5% of supply) was claimable; claims ran June 20–Sept 20, 2024. This is a claim/airdrop mechanism, not an investor sale.
- **No traditional public or private ZRO sale to investors occurred at TGE.** LayerZero Labs states it "has never had to sell a token to fund itself." The nearest token-based capital events are secondary purchases (a16z $55M in April 2025; Citadel/ARK/Tether in Feb 2026) — all purchases of *existing* tokens at market/fair value, not primary raises.

## Details

**Funding trajectory interpretation.** LayerZero's cumulative *priced-equity* funding is $263M (through Series B, April 2023), and it has raised **no new priced equity round since** — a striking fact given it is now (Feb 2026) positioning the Zero L1 for institutional finance. Its post-2023 capital story is almost entirely *token-based and buyback-driven*: rather than diluting via new equity, LayerZero has used Stargate cash flow and (per its own statements) Labs operating profit to *repurchase* ZRO, while blue-chip names (a16z, Citadel, ARK, Tether) have taken positions by buying existing ZRO and/or small equity stakes. This is an unusually self-funded posture for a company launching a capital-intensive L1.

**Why the FTX settlement figure matters and why it is unknowable.** The only primary source is Pellegrino's self-interested X post; the FTX estate never published terms, and no Rule 9019 compromise motion or stipulation disclosing a dollar figure is publicly retrievable. Analysts should treat the settlement as *terms-undisclosed*. The economically meaningful facts: (a) LayerZero returned the "original repurchase" (the value exchanged in the Nov 2022 buyout) to the estate; (b) it returned 40M ZRO to strategic partners; (c) it absorbed "millions" in legal fees; (d) it did not raise capital to do so.

**Revenue reality check.** Despite $225B+ in lifetime cross-chain volume, LayerZero the *protocol* monetizes almost nothing at the Foundation level today — a deliberate "market-share-first" choice. The bull case (Messari's "A Valuation of LayerZero") applies forward fee projections to derive a fully-diluted valuation "range from $290.27 million in the bear case to $3.00 billion in the base case, with $19.11 billion in the bull case," and cautions that ZRO "faces approximately 100% inflation from remaining vesting unlocks through 2030." These are *projections/valuation scenarios*, not realized revenue, and turnout on fee referendums has been low (3.71% in Dec 2025). Realized token-holder value today comes only from Stargate-funded buybacks (~$1.2M in Sept–Nov 2025).

## Recommendations
1. **Anchor "total funding raised" on the $263M cumulative equity figure** (The Block / CB Insights) and explicitly note no priced equity round has occurred since April 2023.
2. **Attach the a16z $55M (April 2025), Stargate acquisition ($110–120M, Aug 2025), 50M-ZRO buyback (~$150M combined-year, Sept 2025), $10M Labs buyback (Nov 2025), and Tether/Citadel/ARK (Feb 2026) events as SEPARATE, non-round capital events** — do not fold them into the round count. Label a16z/Citadel as secondary token purchases; Tether and ARK as equity stakes (ARK also holding tokens).
3. **State the FTX settlement as "amount undisclosed" and explicitly retract the "$111M" figure.** Use the claim amounts ($21.37M / $13.07M / $6.65M; $86M / >$100M framing) only as *sought*, never as *paid*.
4. **For revenue, report the DVN/Executor fee figures (over $11.5M in Q1'24 per Messari; ~$3.6M per DefiLlama) with the critical caveat that these accrue to third parties, not to LayerZero,** and that Foundation protocol revenue is zero pending the fee switch. Report Stargate's $2.4M (Sept–Nov 2025) as the only cash flow reaching the token.
5. **Mark burn rate and current treasury as "not disclosed"** beyond the late-2022 $134M figure and the ~150–165 headcount.
- *Thresholds that would change this:* If a Rule 9019 motion, a new priced round, or an audited treasury disclosure surfaces, revise. If the fee switch activates, protocol revenue becomes a real line item and the Messari valuation scenarios become testable.

## Caveats
- Several figures conflict across sources: Series A ($6M vs $6.3M); Stargate acquisition ($110M reported vs $120M company figure vs a stray "$138M"); and the Sept 2025 buyback (~$150M combined-year per Unchained vs an erroneous "$120M"). Both sides of each conflict are reported above.
- Data-aggregator revenue/headcount/total-funding figures (Getlatka $33.4M; ZoomInfo; Tracxn's $318M "total funding") are **unverified estimates** that conflict with primary sources; the $318M figure appears to double-count secondary token purchases as funding rounds.
- The FTX settlement amount, LayerZero's current (2023–2026) treasury balance, the operating burn rate, and the dollar sizes of the Tether/Citadel/ARK investments are **all not disclosed**.
- Forward-looking fee-switch revenue and Messari's FDV scenarios ($290M–$19.11B) are **projections**, not realized results.
