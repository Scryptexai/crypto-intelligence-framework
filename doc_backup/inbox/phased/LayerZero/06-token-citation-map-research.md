# LayerZero ZRO: Tokenomics & Governance — Sourced Reference Report

*Research date: July 26, 2026. Scope: tokenomics and governance only (equity funding rounds and treasury deliberately excluded, per request). Every fact below carries its source name, date, and the specific claim it supports so citations can be attached individually.*

## TL;DR
- **ZRO is a fixed 1,000,000,000-token supply split four ways** (Community 38.3% / Strategic Partners 32.2% / Core Contributors 25.5% / Tokens Repurchased 4.0%), [Tokenomist](https://tokenomist.ai/layerzero) with Strategic Partners and Core Contributors on identical 3-year vests (1-year cliff, then monthly over 24 months). Buybacks conducted to date are **HELD/re-locked in the Foundation/Labs treasury, NOT burned** — no permanent supply reduction has occurred.
- **The ZRO fee switch has NEVER been activated.** All four semi-annual referendums (Dec 2024, Jun 2025, Dec 2025, Jun 2026) failed to reach quorum and closed with "Outcome: Off," per the LayerZero Foundation's own governance page. Several secondary sources incorrectly claim it activated in December 2025 / February 2026 — this is contradicted by the primary source.
- **The genesis "Tokens Repurchased" 40M bucket and the FTX/Alameda 40M are the SAME tokens**: the LayerZero blog confirms the original TGE allocation included 40M repurchased from FTX/Alameda, which the Foundation returned to strategic partners as part of the Jan 31, 2025 settlement (strategic-partner allocation then netted to 312M).

## Key Findings
1. **Vesting.** Strategic Partners (32.2%) and Core Contributors (25.5%) both follow a **3-year vesting schedule: a 1-year lock (cliff) followed by monthly unlocks over the next 2 years** — confirmed verbatim by the LayerZero Foundation's official "Introducing ZRO" post.
2. **Buyback = HOLD, not burn.** Bought-back ZRO is held/re-locked in treasury; DefiLlama's methodology explicitly states tokens "flow to the Foundation treasury and are not burnt by the contract." Only the (currently inactive) fee switch would burn.
3. **Fee switch = a buyback-AND-BURN mechanism, but dormant.** If ever activated, a Treasury Contract collects the protocol fee on the local chain and burns the ZRO. [Medium](https://info.layerzero.foundation/introducing-zro-d39df554a9b7?gi=254524645d6b) It has not been activated in four attempts.
4. **Governance is narrow:** a single, autonomous, immutable on-chain fee-switch referendum every six months — no treasury/parameter/grants votes at the protocol level as of this date.
5. **Holder concentration is hard to read** because ZRO is an omnichain (OFT) token — Ethereum holds a negligible share and most supply sits on Arbitrum.

### 4. Fee switch — current status, quorum rules, referendum history
- **CURRENT STATUS (as of July 2026): NOT ACTIVATED / OFF.** The LayerZero Foundation Fee Switch page (layerzero.foundation/fee-switch) lists all four votes with "Outcome: Off":
  - **Vote #1 — Dec 20–27, 2024 — Off.** Quorum 60% of circulating supply; [Metaverse Post](https://mpost.io/layerzero-foundation-announces-protocol-fee-switch-referendum-set-for-december-20/) turnout ~10.96% (LayerZero blog).
  - **Vote #2 — Jun 20–27, 2025 — Off.** Quorum lowered to 50.40% (Foundation X, June 2025); turnout ~13.01%.
  - **Vote #3 — Dec 19–27, 2025 — Off.** Quorum 40.59% (~230M ZRO); [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/amp/news/detail/12560605123924) ~97% "Yes" among votes cast but turnout only ~3.71%; failed on quorum. [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/amp/news/detail/12560605123924) Foundation X post (Dec 26–27, 2025): "Since quorum was not met, the LayerZero protocol fee will remain off. The next vote is in six months!"
  - **Vote #4 — Jun 20–27, 2026 — Off.**
- **Dynamic quorum:** since Referendum #2, the Foundation implemented a "dynamic quorum model with a floor at 20%" (fee-switch referendum #2 post, info.layerzero.foundation). Approval has exceeded 96% in the three referendums where it was reported (LayerZero blog).
- **Mechanism if activated: buyback-AND-BURN (permanent supply reduction).** LayerZero Foundation, "Introducing ZRO" (verbatim): "If the fee switch is activated through governance, the referendum Treasury Contract collects fees on the local chain and burns them." [Medium](https://info.layerzero.foundation/introducing-zro-d39df554a9b7?gi=254524645d6b) The Fee Switch page adds: "Collected fees would be converted to ZRO and burned." The fee is capped at the aggregate DVN + Executor cost of a message.
- **MAJOR CONFLICT to flag:** Several secondary sources state the fee switch activated in December 2025 / February 2026 (0xprocessing.com; [0xProcessing](https://0xprocessing.com/blog/zro-token-review/) a CoinMarketCap AI page; some KuCoin explainers). **These are incorrect.** The primary source (Foundation governance page + Foundation X posts, Dec 26–27, 2025) confirms Referendum #3 FAILED on quorum and the fee remains off. DefiLlama corroborates "$0 revenue," and ainvest (July 2026) notes ~$9,652–$11,195/day in fees are NOT flowing back to the token/treasury. Treat any "fee switch is live" claim as unverified/erroneous.

### 5. FTX settlement 40M vs "Tokens Repurchased" 40M — SAME tokens
- **They are the same 40M.** LayerZero blog "The ZRO Token" (June 3, 2026, verbatim): "The original TGE allocation included 40M tokens repurchased from FTX / Alameda Research. LayerZero Foundation returned those tokens to strategic partners as part of the legal settlement. The total strategic partners allocation is now 312M." [LayerZero](https://layerzero.network/blog/the-zro-token)
- **Reconciliation math:** Strategic Partners started at 322M; the Foundation's Sept 2025 buyback removed 50M (→ 272M); the returned 40M FTX/Alameda tokens were added to strategic partners (→ 312M). Net: 322 − 50 + 40 = 312M. Consistent with the blog.
- **Settlement facts:** LayerZero and the FTX estate settled on **January 31, 2025**. CEO **Bryan Pellegrino's Jan. 31, 2025 X post** (verbatim): "After more than two long years and millions in legal fees (lawyers always win) we have reached a settlement agreement with the FTX estate… The original repurchase has been returned to the estate." The dispute traced to 2022 Alameda transactions: per **Cointelegraph (Jan. 31, 2025)**, Alameda sent $70 million to LayerZero and bought $25 million worth of STG tokens, plus a $45M loan LayerZero later sought to forgive in exchange for buying back Alameda's ~5% equity stake. **Clawback figures differ by outlet:** Cointelegraph (Jan. 31, 2025) — "FTX had sought more than $21 million from LayerZero as part of the lawsuit"; CoinGape/TronWeekly — the estate's 2023 suit sought "over $86 million." Report both.

### 6. Governance mechanism
- **Scope is narrow and singular.** ZRO governance today = the semi-annual fee-switch referendum, and nothing else at the protocol level. LayerZero Foundation, "Introducing ZRO" (verbatim): "An immutable voting contract enforces a public onchain referendum every six months, allowing ZRO holders to vote on activating or deactivating the protocol's fee switch." [Medium](https://info.layerzero.foundation/introducing-zro-d39df554a9b7?gi=254524645d6b)
- **Mechanics (verbatim):** "ZRO voting is simple: autonomous, onchain, and immutable — enabling holders to turn on or turn off the fee switch of the protocol by simply signalling from the chain they hold ZRO on." [Medium](https://info.layerzero.foundation/introducing-zro-d39df554a9b7?gi=254524645d6b) Holders vote with their **entire ZRO balance from a single chain** (to prevent double-counting); **LayerZero Labs, the Foundation, and related parties abstain** (Phemex/RootData reporting on Referendum #3, Dec 2025). Only circulating ZRO is eligible to vote. [Metaverse Post](https://mpost.io/layerzero-foundation-announces-protocol-fee-switch-referendum-set-for-december-20/) [X](https://x.com/LayerZero_Core/status/1933624822111875355)
- **Binding?** Yes — the vote directly controls an immutable on-chain contract; it is not merely advisory/Snapshot. (The separate Stargate acquisition vote in 2025 was a distinct Stargate DAO Snapshot process — ~95% approval, >15,100 addresses — and is not part of ZRO's own governance.)
- **Delegation:** the official materials describe direct token-weighted voting and do **not** mention delegation. Some secondary sources (itrusty.io; a 0xprocessing line) claim delegation is supported; this is **not confirmed** by primary docs — treat as unverified.
- **Future scope:** With the "Zero" L1 (targeted fall 2026), ZRO is slated to become the staking + gas + governance asset. CEO Bryan Pellegrino (Feb. 2026) confirmed "There will be no new token for Zero. ZRO is the only asset." Secondary coverage (KuCoin) suggests future votes on protocol upgrades/parameters/grants — but this is prospective, not live.

### 7. Holder concentration (with heavy caveats)
- **STRUCTURAL CAVEAT:** ZRO is an **Omnichain Fungible Token (OFT)**, not a single-chain ERC-20. The Ethereum contract holds a negligible fraction; the bulk of supply resides on **Arbitrum One** (canonical chain). Ethereum-based "top-N holder %" figures are therefore misleading, and cross-chain totals are non-additive due to lock/mint OFT accounting.
- **CoinCarp (Ethereum, observed July 26, 2026):** ~14,109 Ethereum holders; **Top 10 = 0.00%, Top 20/50/100 ≈ 0.01%** [coincarp](https://www.coincarp.com/currencies/layerzero/richlist/) — confirming Ethereum holds almost no ZRO. One address labeled Bitfinex.
- **Arbiscan (Arbitrum One, July 26, 2026):** **456,712 holders**; [arbiscan](https://arbiscan.io/token/0x6985884c4392d348587b19cb9eaaf157f13271cd) ~26.2M ZRO residing on Arbitrum at that time. The precise Top-10/50/100 aggregate percentage table could not be extracted (bot protection + dynamic tables) — flagged as unavailable rather than guessed.
- **Etherscan (Ethereum ERC-20, July 10, 2026 snapshot):** 24,681 holders; ~950.95M reported on the Ethereum contract in one snapshot — do NOT read this as circulating supply given OFT accounting.
- **Named concentration signals:**
  - **Nansen (March 2026):** a single entity accumulated **24.5M ZRO = 2.6% of circulating supply** across **9 wallets funded from Coinbase Prime** (avg entry $1.94; ~$47.5M total; zero sells recorded). Nansen specified **8 of the 9 wallets were funded within a four-hour window on March 9, 2026 ($35.8M moved in a single afternoon)**, with four wallets receiving a 1-ZRO test transaction first — a standard institutional verification step. Nansen's verbatim conclusion (via Coinpedia/crypto.news): "This isn't retail. Large-scale institutional positioning ahead of March 20 unlock ($55M), indicating conviction despite known sell pressure." CEO **Bryan Pellegrino denied any special arrangement** ("no special deals"; investors "encouraged to purchase ZRO on the open market without special deals," per AMBCrypto).
  - **LayerZero blog (data to May 31, 2026):** "**A single entity accounts for 37.9% of all unlocked ZRO sold in the open market to date**" and has sold >80% of its unlocked position — a seller-side concentration signal; that seller's remaining position unlocks at 2.05M/month.
  - **LayerZero Foundation** holds ~183M ZRO re-locked (including the 50M Sept-2025 buyback), released until Zero mainnet.
- **Bottom line:** No clean cross-chain Top-10/50/100 figure is publicly available from Nansen/Arkham/Bubblemaps; the most concrete named figures are Nansen's 2.6%-single-entity accumulation and the Foundation's 37.9%-of-sales concentration.

### 8. Circulating supply & unlock status (major source conflict)
- **CONFLICT — figures differ widely** because trackers treat re-locked Foundation tokens and OFT accounting differently:
  - **CoinGecko (July 2026):** ~350–363M circulating (one CoinGecko page also cites 584.2M "unlocked and in circulation," 282.8M locked, 133M TBD [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/layerzero) — internally inconsistent; flag it).
  - **CoinMarketCap (July 2026):** 363,062,136 ZRO circulating.
  - **Tokenomist.ai / CryptoRank (July 2026):** 353.31M unlocked (35.33% of supply). [Tokenomist](https://tokenomist.ai/layerzero)
  - **DefiLlama:** 252.33M circulating (more conservative).
  - **LayerZero blog (June 3, 2026):** "514M is unlocked. The remaining 486M vests across the LayerZero Foundation, strategic partners, and core contributors." [LayerZero](https://layerzero.network/blog/the-zro-token)
- **Interpretation:** the ~350M vs ~514M gap largely reflects whether Foundation-held/re-locked tokens are counted as "circulating." The Foundation had 250M of its 383M unlocked at launch, then re-locked 183M. Use **~350–363M as the market-facing circulating figure and ~514M as the Foundation's "unlocked" (issued-from-vesting) figure**, and always cite the tracker + date for any single number.
- **Investor unlock status (LayerZero blog, to May 31, 2026):** **134.7M ZRO unlocked to investors since TGE; 85.9M (63.8%) still held; average open-market sales ~4.9M ZRO/month (~0.5% of supply).** The Foundation buyback lowered gross monthly Strategic Partners unlock from 15M to 12.7M.
- **Airdrop:** ~1.28M eligible wallets received ZRO in the June 2024 event (85M / 8.5%); [Datawallet](https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/what-is-layerzero) a later phase redistributed ~10M reclaimed Sybil tokens to active wallets.

### Proof-of-Donation & Sybil defense (baseline verification)
- **Proof-of-Donation:** for June–September 2024, claimants donated **$0.10 per ZRO** in ETH, USDC, or USDT to **Protocol Guild** (Ethereum core-dev funding collective) before claiming the initial 8.5%. (LayerZero Foundation "Introducing ZRO"; corroborated by tokenradar.ai.)
- **Sybil defense (LayerZero Foundation "Introducing ZRO"):** Phase 1 — a 14-day self-report window returning **15% of intended allocation** to those who self-identified; Phase 2 — bounty hunters earned **10% of identified addresses' intended allocation**; run in collaboration with Chaos Labs and Nansen. Net result: **~10,000,000 ZRO (~1% of total supply) saved from Sybil addresses.**

- **Correct the record on two widely-repeated errors:** (1) buybacks are HELD/re-locked, not burned; (2) the fee switch is OFF, not active. Both are contradicted by primary sources despite frequent secondary-source claims to the contrary — treat these as the two highest-risk facts to get right.
- **Treat circulating-supply figures as a range (~350–363M market-facing to ~514M Foundation-"unlocked").** Never publish a single circulating number without naming the tracker and observation date.
- **Monitoring thresholds that would change the picture:** (a) a fee-switch referendum finally clearing its dynamic quorum (≥20% floor) and passing — this would flip ZRO to a live buyback-and-burn asset and begin permanent supply reduction (watch each June/December vote); (b) Zero L1 mainnet launch (targeted fall 2026), which unlocks the Foundation's re-locked 183M and turns ZRO into gas/staking (a supply-side event); (c) any on-chain burn transaction to a null address — none observed to date; its appearance would be the first hard evidence of a burn.

## Caveats
- **Fee-switch "activated" claims are false** per primary sources; several reputable-adjacent outlets published the error. Independent corroboration: DefiLlama shows $0 protocol revenue and the Foundation's own page shows four "Off" outcomes.
- **Circulating-supply and unlock percentages conflict** across CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, Tokenomist, DefiLlama, and the LayerZero blog; ranges are reported rather than a single number, and one CoinGecko page is internally inconsistent.
- **Holder concentration cannot be cleanly quantified cross-chain** due to OFT accounting; Ethereum Etherscan/CoinCarp concentration figures (Top-10 ≈ 0.00%) are unrepresentative because supply sits mainly on Arbitrum, where the Top-N table could not be extracted.
- **Aggregator allocation breakdowns (e.g., tokenomics.com) diverge** from the Foundation's official 38.3 / 32.2 / 25.5 / 4.0 split; the Foundation figures are authoritative.
- **The 4% "Tokens Repurchased" vesting schedule is not officially disclosed;** aggregator schedules (tokenradar.ai, DropsTab) are modeled estimates, not primary disclosures.
- **Delegation in ZRO governance is unverified** — claimed by some secondary sources but absent from primary docs, which describe only direct, single-chain token-weighted voting.
- Several source pages are dated June/July 2026 and reflect a fast-moving situation (Zero L1, institutional investments, Stargate integration); figures may shift after publication.
