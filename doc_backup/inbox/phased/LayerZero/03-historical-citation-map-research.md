# LayerZero (LayerZero Labs Ltd.) — Sourced Citation Map for a 15-Event Intelligence Timeline

## TL;DR
- **Real, URL-backed sources were located for all 15 events, and the single most important disputed fact — the Kelp DAO / rsETH exploit date — resolves cleanly to April 18, 2026 (one incident, not 2024, and not two separate incidents), corroborated by CoinDesk, Chainalysis, The Block, and QuillAudits.**
- Three claims in your source material need correction before publishing: the ~$2M seed round was co-led by Multicoin Capital and Sino Global Capital (not Binance Labs, which led the September 2021 Series A); the funds trapped on FTX were $11.5M (not $10.7M); and the Stargate acquisition was approved by DAO vote on August 25, 2025 with nearly 95% support.
- Five specifics could NOT be verified from any findable source and should be flagged in the timeline: a "23-50585" adversary proceeding; a Motion to Dismiss dated November 20, 2023 with briefing completing March 12, 2024; the Alameda-estate liquidation of 129M STG into ~11.14M ZRO; the "80M+ historical messages" metric; and Ondo Finance's multi-DVN adoption.
