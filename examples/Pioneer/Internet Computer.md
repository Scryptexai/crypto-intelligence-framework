## Identity
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Identity.md`_

- **Project:** Internet Computer (ICP)
- **Category:** Layer 1 — "infinite blockchain" (smart contracts served langsung dari chain, canister smart contracts)
- **Era:** 2021–sekarang (konsep 2015, pre-sale 2017-2018)
- **Founders:** Dominic Williams (DFINITY Foundation, Swiss nonprofit; backers awal a16z/Polychain) (HIGH) [The Block, https://www.theblock.co/post/291215/internet-computer-founder-says-crypto-has-snake-oil-problem]

## Classification
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Classification.md`, `docs/Taxonomy/Sectors.md`_

Layer 1 / canister smart contracts — web-serving on-chain, reverse-gas model.

## Community & Incentive
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Incentives.md`_

Distribusi: pre-sale privat 2017-2018 + public launch Mei 2021; **tidak ada airdrop publik: status Belum ada**. Launch terkenal karena crash: ATH ~$750 (10 Mei 2021) → -90% dalam sebulan; Arkham mengidentifikasi 44 "probable insider addresses" menyetor 10 juta ICP (>$2B saat itu) ke exchange berbarengan dengan penurunan — kasus studi distribusi insider-heavy. (HIGH) [NYT DealBook, https://www.nytimes.com/2021/06/28/business/dealbook/dfinity-icp-ico.html; Flitpay, https://www.flitpay.com/blog/internet-computer-icp-price-prediction]

## Innovation
_ref: `docs/Innovation/`_

Canister smart contracts + chain-key cryptography: hosting aplikasi penuh on-chain (backend on-chain); visi "infinite blockchain" dengan skalabilitas via subnet. (MEDIUM) [The Block, https://www.theblock.co/post/291215/internet-computer-founder-says-crypto-has-snake-oil-problem]

## Evolution Summary
_ref: `docs/TokenLifecycle/`_

Konsep 2015 → pre-sale 2017-2018 → public launch Mei 2021 (market cap >$18B di puncak) → crash -90% sebulan (dilusi token + insider deposits) → ATL ~$1.98 (Okt 2025) → pembangunan subnet & ekosistem berlanjut meski harga tertekan. (HIGH) [NYT DealBook, https://www.nytimes.com/2021/06/28/business/dealbook/dfinity-icp-ico.html; Flitpay, https://www.flitpay.com/blog/internet-computer-icp-price-prediction]

## Why Selected

Kasus ekstrem hype-driven launch + dilusi insider: kontras penting untuk pola distribusi & retensi nilai (banding fair-launch Kaspa vs insider-heavy ICP).
