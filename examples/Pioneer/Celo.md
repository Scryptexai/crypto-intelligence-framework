## Identity
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Identity.md`_

- **Project:** Celo
- **Category:** Layer 1 mobile-first payments → transisi ke Ethereum L2 (2023-2025)
- **Era:** 2020–sekarang (founded 2017)
- **Founders:** Rene Reinsberg, Marek Olszewski (eks-GoDaddy), Sep Kamvar (2017); backing Jack Dorsey dkk. (HIGH) [OKX, https://www.okx.com/en-us/learn/what-is-celo; Nebeus, https://blog.nebeus.com/what-is-celo/]

## Classification
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Classification.md`, `docs/Taxonomy/Sectors.md`_

L1 payments dengan stablecoin native (cUSD, cEUR) + identity berbasis nomor telepon; proposal & migrasi ke Ethereum L2 (2023→2025).

## Community & Incentive
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Incentives.md`_

Distribusi: public sale (CoinList/Coinbase, 2020) + private rounds; insentif inklusi keuangan (program komunitas stablecoin). **Tidak ada airdrop publik besar: status Belum ada** (kampanye kecil stablecoin tidak mengubah status). (MEDIUM) [NDAX, https://ndax.io/en/blog/article/what-is-celo-celo; Nebeus, https://blog.nebeus.com/what-is-celo/]

## Innovation
_ref: `docs/Innovation/`_

Mobile-first: wallet berbasis nomor telepon, stablecoin algoritmik-kolateral (cUSD/cEUR) native L1; lalu pivot arsitektur ke Ethereum L2 — pengakuan bahwa spesialisasi payments lebih kuat sebagai L2 Ethereum. (MEDIUM) [OKX, https://www.okx.com/en-us/learn/what-is-celo]

## Evolution Summary
_ref: `docs/TokenLifecycle/`_

Founding 2017 (SF/Berlin) → funding rounds (Dorsey dkk.) → mainnet April 2020 + stablecoin cUSD → peak perhatian inklusi keuangan 2021 → proposal pindah ke Ethereum L2 (2023) → migrasi bertahap 2024-2025. (MEDIUM) [NDAX, https://ndax.io/en/blog/article/what-is-celo-celo; Nebeus, https://blog.nebeus.com/what-is-celo/]

## Why Selected

Kasus pivot L1→L2 (menyerahkan sovereignty konsensus) — pola adaptasi arsitektur yang semakin umum di era konsolidasi L2.
