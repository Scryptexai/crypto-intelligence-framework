(HIGH) [Messari, https://messari.io/project/pendle/profile]

## Innovation
_ref: `docs/Innovation/`_

Pemisahan aset yield-bearing menjadi Principal Token (PT, fixed rate) dan Yield Token (YT, variabel) dengan AMM khusus; dominan 50-60% pasar yield tokenization; ekspansi ke funding-rate trading via Boros. (MEDIUM) [Datawallet, https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/what-is-pendle-finance]

## Evolution Summary
_ref: `docs/TokenLifecycle/`_

Benchmark (Nov 2020) → testnet 20 Maret 2021 → liquidity bootstrapping PENDLE 28 April 2021 → mainnet Ethereum 16 Juni 2021 (mint YT/OT untuk aUSDC/cDAI) → ekspansi Avalanche (Nov 2021) → integrasi LST (stETH) & restaking wave 2023-2024 (TVL ~$230M awal 2023 → puncak ~$8.9B Agustus 2025) → Ethena USDe integration. (MEDIUM) [Messari, https://messari.io/project/pendle/profile; Datawallet, https://www.datawallet.com/crypto/what-is-pendle-finance]

## Why Selected

Evolusi kategori yield-tokenization dari niche ke infrastruktur fixed-rate DeFi; kasus studi demand-driven growth yang mengikuti gelombang LST/restaking.
