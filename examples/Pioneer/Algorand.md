## Classification
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Classification.md`, `docs/Taxonomy/Sectors.md`_

Layer 1 / PPoS — finalitas instan, throughput tinggi, biaya rendah.

## Community & Incentive
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Incentives.md`_

Distribusi awal: Dutch auction 19 Juni 2019 (25 juta ALGO) + alokasi public sale 30%, node-running grants 25%, team+investor 20%, participation rewards 17.5%, foundation 5%, end-user grants 2.5% (MEDIUM) [99bitcoins, https://99bitcoins.com/cryptocurrency/what-is-algorand/]. Participation rewards (hold-to-earn) kemudian bergeser ke governance program & staking rewards — **bukan airdrop publik: status airdrop Belum ada**.

## Innovation
_ref: `docs/Innovation/`_

Pure Proof-of-Stake dengan cryptographic sortition (validator acak berbobot stake) — finalitas <5 detik tanpa forking; prioritas skalabilitas-trilema via desain, bukan roadmap bertahap. (MEDIUM) [Grokipedia, https://grokipedia.com/page/Algorand]

## Evolution Summary
_ref: `docs/TokenLifecycle/`_

Whitepaper 2017 → testnet Juni 2018 → mainnet + Dutch auction Juni 2019 → participation rewards era awal → transisi ke governance rewards → staking rewards (upgrade besar) → vesting foundation berlanjut hingga ~2030. (MEDIUM) [Grokipedia, https://grokipedia.com/page/Algorand; Medium, https://medium.com/@powertrade_options/algorand-options-unleashed-high-speed-algo-trading-with-advanced-strategies-on-powertrade-ee850edb7dd8]

## Why Selected

Kasus L1 akademis-first dengan distribusi auction (bukan airdrop/ICO biasa) dan evolusi insentif hold→governance→staking — kontras pola distribusi vs L1 era airdrop.
