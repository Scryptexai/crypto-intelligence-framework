## Identity
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Identity.md`_

- **Project:** Hedera
- **Category:** Layer 1 enterprise — Hashgraph (aBFT DAG consensus), council-governed
- **Era:** 2019–sekarang (founding 2017)
- **Founders:** Leemon Baird (inventor Hashgraph, 2016) & Mance Harmon (2017/2018) (HIGH) [Hedera Council FAQ, https://hederacouncil.org/faq; Upay, https://blog.upay.com/crypto-terminology/hedera-hbar/]

## Classification
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Classification.md`, `docs/Taxonomy/Sectors.md`_

Layer 1 / enterprise DLT — BFT finality 3-5 detik, fee ~$0.0001; governance oleh Hedera Governing Council (hingga 39 perusahaan: Google, IBM, Boeing, Deutsche Telekom, Ubisoft, dll.).

## Community & Incentive
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Incentives.md`_

Distribusi via SAFT sale (proceeds diperlakukan sebagai deferred revenue); distribusi HBAR ke pemegang SAFT mulai 16 September 2019 beberapa jam setelah Open Access; SAFT Exchange Offer 2020 (distribusi kuartalan); final lump-sum distribution awal 2025. Alokasi: founders 13.8%, Swirlds/Hashgraph 8%. **Tidak ada airdrop publik: status Belum ada.** (HIGH) [Hedera Council FAQ, https://hederacouncil.org/faq; Reddit r/Hedera, https://www.reddit.com/r/Hedera/comments/1fn9swo/how_much_hbar_does_leemon_baird_and_mance_harmon/]

## Innovation
_ref: `docs/Innovation/`_

Hashgraph: leaderless aBFT DAG consensus (gossip-about-gossip + virtual voting) — finalitas cepat tanpa mining; model governance council korporat sebagai jalan desentralisasi bertahap (2022: council membeli IP hashgraph dari Swirlds dengan non-compete). (HIGH) [Upay, https://blog.upay.com/crypto-terminology/hedera-hbar/; Hedera Council FAQ, https://hederacouncil.org/faq]

## Evolution Summary
_ref: `docs/TokenLifecycle/`_

Hashgraph invented 2016 → Hedera founded 2017 → mainnet Open Access Sep 2019 (5 council) → council tumbuh ke 39 (Google/IBM/Boeing, 2020-2021) → ATH HBAR ~$0.57 (2021) → pembelian IP hashgraph oleh council 2022 + Swirlds Labs & HBAR Foundation → SAFT settlement final 2025. (HIGH) [Upay, https://blog.upay.com/crypto-terminology/hedera-hbar/; Hedera Council FAQ, https://hederacouncil.org/faq]

## Why Selected

Pola distribusi enterprise (SAFT + council) vs fair launch vs airdrop — spektrum lengkap untuk analisis governance & desentralisasi bertahap.
