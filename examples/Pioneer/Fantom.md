## Identity
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Identity.md`_

- **Project:** Fantom (FTM) → rebrand **Sonic (S)** 2024-2025
- **Category:** Layer 1 — Lachesis aBFT DAG, EVM-compatible
- **Era:** 2019–sekarang (Foundation 2018; Michael Kong CEO)
- **Founders:** Fantom Foundation (2018); tokoh awal Ahn Byung-Ik (naratif founder kontroversial); Michael Kong CEO (MEDIUM) [Dextools, https://www.dextools.io/tutorials/what-is-sonic-network-s-token-fantom-rebrand-guide-2026]

## Classification
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Classification.md`, `docs/Taxonomy/Sectors.md`_

Layer 1 / aBFT DAG — finalitas cepat, fee rendah; DeFi summer 2.0 hub (TVL puncak >$12B, 2021).

## Community & Incentive
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Incentives.md`_

Distribusi awal: private sale 2018 (ERC-20 FTM) + listing 2019; staking rewards; insentif ekosistem era DeFi summer (grants agresif). Era Sonic: **FeeM (Fee Monetization) membayar developer hingga 90% fee jaringan**. **Tidak ada airdrop publik besar: status Belum ada.** (MEDIUM) [Dextools, https://www.dextools.io/tutorials/what-is-sonic-network-s-token-fantom-rebrand-guide-2026; BeInCrypto, https://beincrypto.com/learn/fantom-crypto/]

## Innovation
_ref: `docs/Innovation/`_

Lachesis aBFT (leaderless, DAG, finalitas asinkron) sejak Opera 2019; migrasi penuh ke Sonic L1 (testnet Sep 2024, mainnet 18 Des 2024) dengan swap FTM→S 1:1 — rebrand + token swap penuh sebagai strategi refresh. (HIGH) [Grokipedia, https://grokipedia.com/page/Fantom_to_Sonic_migration; Dextools, https://www.dextools.io/tutorials/what-is-sonic-network-s-token-fantom-rebrand-guide-2026]

## Evolution Summary
_ref: `docs/TokenLifecycle/`_

Foundation 2018 → testnet akhir 2019 → Opera mainnet Des 2019 (EVM) → DeFi summer 2.0 TVL >$12B (2021) → bear 2022-2023 → rebrand Sonic/Sonic Labs 2024 → Sonic testnet Sep 2024 → Sonic mainnet 18 Des 2024 / Januari 2025 + migrasi 1:1 + FeeM. (HIGH) [Grokipedia, https://grokipedia.com/page/Fantom_to_Sonic_migration; Dextools, https://www.dextools.io/tutorials/what-is-sonic-network-s-token-fantom-rebrand-guide-2026]
