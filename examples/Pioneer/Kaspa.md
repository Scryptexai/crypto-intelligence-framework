## Identity
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Identity.md`_

- **Project:** Kaspa
- **Category:** Layer 1 — Proof-of-Work BlockDAG (GHOSTDAG), 10 blok/detik
- **Era:** 2021–sekarang
- **Founders:** Yonatan Sompolinsky (peneliti GHOSTDAG/PHANTOM; riset sejak 2013, paper 2013 dgn Aviv Zohar); DAGLabs (2017, backing Polychain) dibubarkan pasca-launch untuk model komunitas (HIGH) [Grokipedia, https://grokipedia.com/page/Kaspa's; BYDFi, https://www.bydfi.com/en/cointalk/kaspa-wallets-how-to-use]

## Classification
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Classification.md`, `docs/Taxonomy/Sectors.md`_

Layer 1 / PoW BlockDAG — payments-first; smart contracts direncanakan via L2/Rust (Toccata fork 2026).

## Community & Incentive
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Incentives.md`_

**Fair launch murni 7 November 2021: tanpa premine, tanpa ICO, tanpa VC, tanpa alokasi founder — 100% KAS via mining (kHeavyHash), halving tahunan smooth; max supply 28.7B.** Status airdrop: Belum ada (distribusi sepenuhnya mining). (HIGH) [Coinstancy, https://coinstancy.com/academy/guides/kaspa/; Grokipedia, https://grokipedia.com/page/Kaspa's]

## Innovation
_ref: `docs/Innovation/`_

GHOSTDAG: generalisasi Nakamoto Consensus yang mengizinkan blok paralel (blockDAG) dengan blue-set finality — konfirmasi ~10 detik tanpa membuang orphan blocks; penerapan akademis-ke-mainnet langka. (HIGH) [Coinstancy, https://coinstancy.com/academy/guides/kaspa/]

## Evolution Summary
_ref: `docs/TokenLifecycle/`_

Riset 2013 (Sompolinsky & Zohar) → DAGLabs 2017 → mainnet fair launch 7 Nov 2021 (genesis 8 Nov) → pertumbuhan hashrate komunitas → roadmap smart contracts (Toccata, Juni 2026) transisi payments→programmable. (MEDIUM) [BYDFi, https://www.bydfi.com/en/cointalk/kaspa-wallets-how-to-use; Grokipedia, https://grokipedia.com/page/Kaspa's]
