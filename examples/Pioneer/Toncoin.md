## Identity
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Identity.md`_

- **Project:** Toncoin (The Open Network)
- **Category:** Layer 1 — PoS (asal: PoW mining Giver contracts), integrasi Telegram
- **Era:** 2020–sekarang (asal proyek Telegram 2018)
- **Founders:** Nikolai & Pavel Durov (Telegram, 2018); dilanjutkan komunitas + TON Foundation pasca-2020 (HIGH) [CCN, https://www.ccn.com/education/crypto/what-is-toncoin-ton-role-functionality-open-network-explained/]

## Classification
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Classification.md`, `docs/Taxonomy/Sectors.md`_

Layer 1 / payments + mini-apps (Telegram @wallet, TON DNS/Storage).

## Community & Incentive
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Incentives.md`_

Distribusi unik: private sale Gram $1.7B (2018) dibatalkan SEC Mei 2020; Telegram mengunci token di "Giver" contracts; **98.5% dari 5 miliar supply ditambang publik via PoW Giver (Juli 2020–Juni 2022, ~200k TON/hari)** — distribusi organik ala Bitcoin, tanpa ICO/IEO pasca-exit. **Tidak ada airdrop: status Belum ada.** (HIGH) [Reflexivity Research, https://www.reflexivityresearch.com/all-reports/telegram-open-network-ton-overview; Bitget Academy, https://www.bitget.site/academy/what-is-toncoin-ton-telegram-history-tokenomics-price-prediction-news]

## Innovation
_ref: `docs/Innovation/`_

Catchchain-based PoS dengan sharding dinamis; kasus langka proyek korporat yang disita regulasi lalu diambil alih open-source dan berhasil mainnet (Mei 2021, rebrand Gram→Toncoin). (MEDIUM) [CCN, https://www.ccn.com/education/crypto/what-is-toncoin-ton-role-functionality-open-network-explained/]

## Evolution Summary
_ref: `docs/TokenLifecycle/`_

Gram sale 2018 → testnet 2019 → SEC halt & Telegram exit Mei 2020 → community mining Juli 2020 → mainnet komunitas Mei 2021 → listing 2021-2022 → integrasi Telegram @wallet Juni 2022 → mining berakhir Juni 2022 → era mini-apps/Notcoin-adjacent growth 2023-2024. (HIGH) [CCN, https://www.ccn.com/education/crypto/what-is-toncoin-ton-role-functionality-open-network-explained/; Bitget Academy, https://www.bitget.site/academy/what-is-toncoin-ton-telegram-history-tokenomics-price-prediction-news]

## Why Selected

Satu-satunya kasus L1 besar yang lahir dari kegagalan regulasi korporat lalu tumbuh via mining publik + distribusi platform Telegram — pola distribusi unik di dataset.
