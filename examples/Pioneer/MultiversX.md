## Community & Incentive
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Incentives.md`_

## Innovation
_ref: `docs/Innovation/`_

## Evolution Summary
_ref: `docs/TokenLifecycle/`_

(MEDIUM) [Giottus, https://www.giottus.com/prediction-price/multiversx; Grokipedia, https://grokipedia.com/page/multiversx]
