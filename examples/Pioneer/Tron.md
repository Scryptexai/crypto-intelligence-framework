## Community & Incentive
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Incentives.md`_

ICO 31 Agustus–2 September 2017: 40 miliar TRX (40% supply) terjual, ~$70 juta (15.200 BTC) di $0.0019/TRX — tepat sebelum larangan ICO Tiongkok; 45% supply ke Justin Sun + TRON Foundation (konsentrasi dikritik). **Tidak ada airdrop: status Belum ada.** (HIGH) [HTX, https://square.htx.com/tron-nasdaq-listing-journey-2025/; CoinCodex, https://coincodex.com/crypto/tron/]

## Innovation
_ref: `docs/Innovation/`_

Bukan inovasi teknis first-mover — inovasi distribusi/regulatori (timing ICO pre-ban) + akuisisi BitTorrent (Juli 2018) untuk distribusi pengguna; kemudian menangkap niche stablecoin settlement setelah larangan kompetitor. (MEDIUM) [crypto.com, https://crypto.com/us/coins-ai/tron/what-is]

## Evolution Summary
_ref: `docs/TokenLifecycle/`_

ICO 2017 (ERC-20) → mainnet Odyssey 2.0 Mei/Juni 2018 + migrasi ke native TRX → akuisisi BitTorrent Juli 2018 → pertumbuhan sebagai venue USDT → gugatan SEC 2023 (TRX/BTT unregistered securities + tuduhan wash trading) berlanjut; Nasdaq listing journey 2025. (HIGH) [HTX, https://square.htx.com/tron-nasdaq-listing-journey-2025/; CoinCodex, https://coincodex.com/crypto/tron/]

## Why Selected

Kasus distribusi terpusat (45% insider) + kelangsungan hidup jangka panjang via product-market-fit stablecoin — kontras pola distribusi vs fair launch (Kaspa).
