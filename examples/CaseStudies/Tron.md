## Technology Intelligence
_ref: `docs/Ontology/Technology.md`_

Consensus Mechanism: Delegated Proof-of-Stake (voting SR).
Security Model: Keamanan = set SR ter voting; kritik sentralisasi (SR terbatas & pengaruh founder); track record uptime tinggi.
